# Quadra AI usage policy

Contributions that are assisted with generative AI are permitted, but we expect a human overseeing and reviewing the generated code. DO NOT let the AI agent co-author or author its own commits, as the contributor is expected to make and author their own commits, and be fully responsible of all the changes.

To ensure self-review and full understanding of the changes, it is strongly recommended to not use generative AI to write the PR description. (unless translation tools are needed)
