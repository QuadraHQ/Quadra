## Fishstrap

The in-game overlay (floating panels, dock and pinning, as drawn in Fishstrap's unfinished `feature/overlay` branch) is inspired by [Fishstrap](https://github.com/fishstrap/fishstrap). Quadra's overlay is a separate implementation written in Rust with GTK4, drawn as its own Wayland layer-shell window on top of Roblox without touching Roblox's memory.
